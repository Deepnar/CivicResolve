## How to Use

### Opening the Chat Assistant
1. Look for the blue chat icon (💬) in the bottom-right corner of any page
2. Click the icon to open the chat dialog
3. The assistant will greet you with context-specific information

### Asking Questions
- Type your question in the input field at the bottom
- Press Enter or click the Send button
- The AI will provide a comprehensive, helpful response
- Ask follow-up questions for clarification

### Using Quick Help Topics
- Look for "Quick help" suggestions below the context badges
- Click any suggested topic to automatically ask that question
- These topics are tailored to your current page and activities

### Managing the Chat Window
- **Minimize**: Click the minimize button to hide the chat while keeping it accessible
- **Maximize**: Click to restore the full chat interface
- **Close**: Click the X to close the chat entirely

## Advanced Features

### Context Detection
The assistant automatically understands:
- **Current Page**: Knows whether you're on the map, reporting an issue, or viewing your profile
- **Active Filters**: Aware of any filters you've applied to issue lists
- **Issue Context**: If viewing a specific issue, knows the issue ID and details
- **User Role**: Provides role-appropriate assistance (citizen vs. administrator)

### Smart Responses
The AI provides:
- **Step-by-step instructions** for complex tasks
- **Feature explanations** with practical examples
- **Best practices** for civic engagement
- **Troubleshooting help** for common issues
- **Policy guidance** for community standards

### Learning and Adaptation
- The assistant learns from community patterns
- Responses improve based on common questions
- Context awareness becomes more refined over time

## Privacy and Data

### What Information is Shared
- Your current page location within CivicResolve
- Your user role (citizen, moderator, admin)
- Context about active filters or current issue being viewed
- The messages you send to the assistant

### What Information is NOT Shared
- Personal information from your profile
- Private messages or communications
- Specific issue details unless you explicitly mention them
- Other users' private data

### Data Handling
- All conversations are processed securely
- No conversation history is permanently stored
- Each chat session is independent and private
- AI responses are generated in real-time

## Troubleshooting

### Chat Assistant Not Responding
1. Check your internet connection
2. Refresh the page and try again
3. Ensure you're logged in to your CivicResolve account
4. Contact support if the issue persists

### Unclear or Incorrect Responses
1. Rephrase your question with more specific details
2. Provide context about what you're trying to accomplish
3. Ask for clarification or examples
4. Report persistent issues to the development team

### Feature Suggestions
The assistant can help you:
- Suggest new features for the platform
- Provide feedback on current functionality
- Connect you with the appropriate channels for feature requests
